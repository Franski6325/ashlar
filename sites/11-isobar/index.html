Methodology

How the contours are born.

Transparent assimilation, signed products, and operator-grade confidence — the stack behind every Isobar plate.

Surface SYNOP, buoy, METAR, AMDAR, and radiosonde reports land in a shared ingest bus. Each observation gets a trust score from instrument class, latency, and neighbor disagreement.

  • Gross-error gates on pressure, temperature, and wind.
  • Buddy checks within 150 km for MSLP outliers.
  • Blacklist / greylist with automatic rehab windows.

ISO-CORE 3.2 runs a hybrid ensemble–variational update on a 9 km North Atlantic mesh, nudging the background toward trusted observations every 90 seconds for the live plate.

  • Background from a 51-member short-range ensemble.
  • Localized covariance to keep coastal gradients sharp.
  • Signed analysis increments stored for audit replay.

Isobars are traced on the analysis grid with 2 hPa intervals by default. Isallobars (3 h tendency) and gradient severity tiles are derived fields, not separate models.

  • Contour topology preserved across refresh to avoid flicker.
  • Gradient severity normalized over 100 km corridors.
  • Vector tendency glyphs optional on operator plates.

Every forecast tile carries member spread, cluster vote, and flip risk. Operators see when the desk should trust a single narrative versus hold for the next cycle.

  • k-means on MSLP anomaly patterns for scenario labels.
  • Flip risk = probability the dominant cluster changes in 6 h.
  • Skill scores published weekly against verifying analyses.

Products are content-addressed and signed. Replay any plate with the exact observation set and model revision that produced it — required for regulated energy and aviation desks.

  • Ed25519 signatures on contour product bundles.
  • Immutable ingest ledger with 90-day hot retention.
  • Role-based desk access with field-scoped API keys.
