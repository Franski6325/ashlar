05 — Cool stone

A well room
without mythology.

Thick walls on the shaded side. Cold plunge. Treatments that name what they do — salt, pressure, quiet — and stop there.

House well

Plunge, steam, still air

Open daily to all guests. Book a treatment or simply take the cold plunge between shade hours. No spa music. No scented fog.

06 — The crossing

Eighteen minutes
if the wind agrees.

No road ends at Atoll. You leave the mainland quay by boat — flat water most days, a wetter story when the monsoon leans in.

How you arrive

Mainland quay meets every scheduled ferry and private transfer. Bags go on first; guests sit under the shade canopy. The lagoon is usually glass by late morning.
