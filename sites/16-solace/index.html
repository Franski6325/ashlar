Modes

How it works

A ritual, not a dashboard.

Open Solace, choose a tempo, and let the session hold the edges. You return when the soft chime says it’s time.

Choose your tempo

Pick Focus, Rest, or Wind-down. Each mode shapes sound, light, and length to match the moment.

Settle into the room

The interface softens. Optional breath cues and ambient textures keep you present without demanding taps.

Return on the chime

A gentle close ends the session. Log a one-word note if you like — or simply step back into your day.
