How Plotline stays honest

04 / 06 Standards
Newsroom rules

Charts fail when the story cheats.

Plotline encodes editorial standards into the frame: zero baselines for bars, labeled uncertainty, source lines that survive export.

0Truncated bars
100%Source stamped
2Key embargo
12Export formats
Column and bar charts start at zero unless an editor explicitly overrides with a documented reason. Overrides stamp a red note on the exported frame.
  • Automatic lint on truncated axes
  • Print proofing checklist before ship
  • Audit log retained for 18 months
Confidence bands, margins of error, and sample notes sit on the canvas — not buried in a caption CMS field. Readers see the fog with the figure.
  • Survey MOE templates
  • Model uncertainty presets
  • Footnote sync to CMS slug
Election night and earnings desks can lock a frame under embargo. Two editors must confirm before the public CDN path opens — no accidental early posts.
Tabular figures, locked leading, and column-rule layouts export to PDF without reflow surprises. Mono labels stay mono; serif titles stay serif.
