Section 02 — Our position

01/ 04
Colophon

Set in Newsreader and Archivo. Printed to screen in Lisbon, four times a week.

A magazine is infrastructure. It should be built to last, maintained in public, and paid for by the people who read it — not by the people who would rather interrupt it.

Since 2016

No programmatic advertising has ever run on this site. Not once, not as a test.

Corrections

Every correction is dated, signed and left in place. The archive is not quietly edited.

Ownership

Folio Press is owned by its eleven staff and no one else. There is no holding company.

Principle 01

Nothing interrupts the reading.

No pop-ups, no auto-play, no consent maze, no newsletter modal three paragraphs in. The page you open is the page you get.

  • Zero third-party trackers
  • 41 kb median page weight
Principle 02

The editor's name is on it.

Every piece carries a writer, an editor and a fact-checker. If you disagree with a call we made, you know exactly whose desk to write to.

  • Three-name byline standard
  • Public editorial guidelines
Principle 03

Corrections in public, forever.

We publish what we got wrong on the same page, at the same size, with the date we learned it. The archive stays as it was written.

  • 63 corrections logged
  • Permanent URLs since 2016
Principle 04

Readers pay. Readers decide.

Membership covers 96% of what it costs to run this magazine. The remaining 4% is print. That arithmetic is the whole editorial policy.

  • Annual budget published
  • Cancel in one click
