03 — Field

Site notes

Observations from mock-ups, quarries, and pours. Dense, dated, and filed for the next plate.

18 Jul 26

Basalt mock-up: joint shadow under diffuse north light

At 6mm open joint, the reveal reads as a continuous graphite line from 8m. Closing to 4mm collapses the elevation into a monolithic plane—useful for interiors, wrong for this façade.

Mock-up
02 Jun 26

Board-formed pour: moisture banding at mid-height

Third lift showed a pale band where retarders overlapped. Spec now calls for single-batch face mix and documented vibration schedule per wall bay.

Concrete
21 Apr 26

Quarry visit — Pietra Blue selection lot B

Vein direction inconsistent across crates 14–19. We re-sorted on site and marked bedding planes with chalk before crate close. Photo plate filed under QR-L15/LOT-B.

Quarry
09 Mar 26

Rainscreen baffle whistling at corner returns

Wind tunnel note: open baffles at external corners produced audible flutter above 18 m/s. Spec revision adds denser mesh at returns and a secondary drip.

Envelope
14 Jan 26

Terrazzo polish schedule vs. slip rating

400 grit held R10 on dry; wet dropped below target. Cap polish at 200 for lobby floors; reserve 400 for vertical panels only.
