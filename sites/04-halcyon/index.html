01 — Thesis

Measured at its worst hour

Peak capability is easy to demonstrate and hard to trust. We index on the tail.

Most intelligence is measured at its best moment. We measure it at its worst hour — the ambiguous prompt, the ninth tool call, the afternoon the traffic doubles.

Calm is not a mood. It is a property you can instrument, bound, and defend in review. Which makes it a property you can build.

Method

Every claim ships with its evaluation harness, seed set, and the runs that disagreed with us. Reproduction should cost an afternoon, not a quarter.

Scope

Nine teams across two cities. Interpretability and evaluation sit in the same room as systems, because a metric no one can serve is a metric no one keeps.

Posture

We publish negative results at the same cadence as positive ones. Three model families are open-weight; the evaluation ledger is public and updated hourly.

04 — Open questions

Five things we do not yet know.

A model can decline for the right reason, the wrong reason, or no reason it can name. We can already trace which features fire during a refusal; what we cannot yet do is issue a certificate that survives paraphrase, translation, and a fresh checkpoint.

Our current best result holds across paraphrase but degrades sharply under translation into low-resource languages — a gap we suspect is about tokenisation, not values.

TeamAlignment
PaperHAL-0039
StatusPreprint

Aggregate recall stays flat deep into the window, which is exactly why the number is misleading. Loss is not uniform: negations, dates, and the second-most-recent instruction go first, and they go quietly.

We are building a forgetting curve per semantic class rather than per token position. Early data suggests position matters far less than whether a fact was ever contradicted.

TeamLong context
PaperHAL-0041
StatusIn review

Most interpretability work happens after the fact, on a laptop, with a model that is no longer in production. Quietfield is our argument that it belongs in the serving path, where it costs something and therefore has to be worth something.

The open question is budget. Two percent overhead is defensible; the honest number once feature cardinality grows past twenty thousand is not yet two percent.

TeamSystems
PaperHAL-0036
StatusPublished

Aviation and medicine both learned to write a document that says why a system is safe enough to fly. Our field writes model cards, which are closer to marketing than to argument.

We are drafting a safety case template with explicit cost: the evidence it requires, the runs it commissions, and the hours a reviewer must spend. If the number is uncomfortable, that is information too.

TeamGovernance
PaperHAL-0042
StatusDraft

The word carries the whole lab, so it had better mean something measurable. Our working definition has three parts: variance under repetition, graceful degradation under load, and legibility of failure.

All three are measurable in isolation. Composing them into a single index without losing the reason each one mattered is the part we have not solved.
