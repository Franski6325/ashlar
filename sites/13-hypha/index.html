04 — Notes

Lab notes
from the bench.

Field and chamber logs published weekly. Raw observations preferred over polished abstracts.

28 Jul 26

Flax hurds outperform alder at high RH

In G-04, T. versicolor on flax reached 92% surface cover in 9 days at 85% RH / 23°C — alder lagged by ~36 hours with denser but slower hyphal fronts.

N. Okada Substrate
19 Jul 26

Night drop protocol reduces contamination

A 4°C overnight thermal drop (26→22) cut bacterial blooms on coffee parchment bags without slowing colonization velocity past day 5.

S. Merle Kinetics
08 Jul 26

New isolate from Cascadia nurse log — HYP-112

White-rot morphotype collected at 780m. ITS pending. Early plate growth suggests aggressive lignin preference; queued for foam trials.

A. Ruiz Field
26 Jun 26

Leather-analog peel strength hits spec

F. fomentarius mat on hemp scrim: peel 2.1 N/cm after 14d press-dry. Soft-goods partner review scheduled for August.
